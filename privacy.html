PulseAndPixelNet Pipeline

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 16, 2026

PulseAndPixelNet Pipeline ("the App") is a personal automation tool built and operated by Şeref Dağ to publish short-form video content that the operator creates to social accounts the operator owns and controls (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok accounts under the PulseAndPixelNet name).

What the App accesses

The App connects only to accounts belonging to its operator, using API credentials (access tokens) issued directly to the operator by each platform. It uses this access to:

  • Upload video files, captions, titles, and hashtags that the operator has produced
  • Check the status of an upload it has just submitted

The App does not browse, collect, or store content, profile information, or activity belonging to any other account or user.

Data storage

Access tokens and related credentials are stored locally on the operator's own computer, in a file the operator controls. They are never transmitted to any third party or server other than the platform that issued them.

No third-party sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties, and does not use platform data for advertising or profiling.

Retention and deletion

Credentials remain on the operator's computer until the operator revokes access from the platform's own settings or deletes the local file. No data is retained anywhere else.
