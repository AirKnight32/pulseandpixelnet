Privacy Policy

Pulse & Pixel Net – PulseAndPixelNet Pipeline · Last updated: 26 September 2026

PulseAndPixelNet Pipeline is a private tool used only by the owner of the Pulse & Pixel Net channels to publish and manage the channel's own videos. It is not offered to the public and no other users can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

Through the YouTube API Services, with the channel owner's permission, the app:

  • uploads videos, thumbnails and descriptions to the owner's own YouTube channel and adds them to playlists;
  • reads comments on the channel's own videos and posts replies as the channel;
  • reads view and performance statistics (YouTube Analytics) of the channel's own videos.

How data is used and stored

This data is used only to run the channel: publishing videos, replying to comments and showing performance statistics to the owner. It is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Other platforms

The same videos are also posted to the channel's own Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts through their official interfaces. No viewer data from these platforms is sold or shared.
